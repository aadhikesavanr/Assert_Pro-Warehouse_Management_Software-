Why a warehouse system instead of spreadsheets

Every stock movement recorded the same way, every time

A warehouse lives or dies on accuracy. Once three people edit the same stock sheet, quantities drift, bins get lost, and orders ship short. AssertPro keeps one record per product, per location, with a full movement history behind it.

  • Live quantity per product — available, reserved, reorder level
  • Inbound and outbound movements logged against every SKU
  • Bin / zone mapping so pickers always know where to go
  • Low-stock and out-of-stock alerts without manual counting
  • Role-based access — admins manage users, staff manage stock
